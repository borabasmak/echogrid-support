EchoGrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026
Publisher: Bora Başmak
Privacy contact: borabasmak3@gmail.com

Overview

EchoGrid is an offline-first puzzle game. The EchoGrid publisher does not operate an account system, advertising service, analytics service, or game backend, and the application code does not transmit puzzle activity or personal information to the publisher.

Information stored on the device

EchoGrid stores puzzle progress and preferences locally on the device. This can include the current board, pings, markers, scores, completion history, streaks, tutorial progress, accessibility preferences, sound and haptic settings, and a backup of an unreadable save when corruption recovery is needed.

This information is used only to operate the game, restore an in-progress puzzle, and show local statistics. It is not uploaded to an EchoGrid server.

Purchases and subscriptions

Optional Explorer Club purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process Apple Account, payment, transaction, subscription-status, renewal, cancellation, and refund information under Apple's policies. EchoGrid receives StoreKit product information and cryptographically verified entitlement or transaction status needed to unlock purchased features. The publisher does not receive payment-card details.

User-initiated sharing

If the player chooses to share a spoiler-free result, iOS presents the system share sheet. Information is sent only to the destination the player selects and is then handled under that destination's privacy terms. EchoGrid does not automatically upload shared results.

Advertising, analytics, sale, and tracking

EchoGrid contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking code. The publisher does not sell personal information and does not use puzzle activity for advertising or profiling.

Sharing with third parties

The publisher does not share locally stored puzzle data with third parties. Apple services such as StoreKit operate under Apple's terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until the player uses Settings → Reset all local progress or removes the app. Reset removes the active save, settings, completion history, statistics, and any corrupt-save recovery backup stored by EchoGrid. Removing the app removes its local application data according to iOS behavior.

Resetting EchoGrid does not delete purchase history or subscription records held by Apple. Players can manage subscriptions through Settings → Legal & Support → Manage Subscription and manage Apple-held information through the relevant Apple Account controls.

Children's privacy

EchoGrid does not knowingly request names, email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information from players.

Changes to this policy

The publisher may update this policy when EchoGrid's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published. App Store privacy disclosures and the in-app link must be kept consistent with the current policy.

Support email and this website

If you email support, Bora Başmak receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email is handled through Gmail under Google’s Privacy Policy. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details. You can request deletion of your support correspondence using the contact address below, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

The privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address, to operate and secure its service under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. These pages contain no publisher-added analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.
