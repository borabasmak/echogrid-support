EchoGrid Support

A calm puzzle about reading echoes and finding hidden beacons.

Need help?

Email borabasmak3@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, EchoGrid version, and what happened. Screenshots are optional. Never send passwords or payment-card details.

Continue a puzzle

Open Play and choose the resume action. EchoGrid saves one unfinished puzzle at a time. Replacing it requires confirmation.

Understand a clue

A number is the distance to the nearest beacon, counting horizontal and vertical steps. Arrows show directions toward the nearest beacon or beacons. Tap a revealed clue and choose “Explain this clue” for examples.

Daily scores and retries

The daily puzzle changes at 00:00 UTC. Your first attempt is eligible for a recorded score. Hitting a beacon ends that attempt; retries and replays are unscored. There is no time penalty.

Restore Explorer access

Use Settings → Legal & Support → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. A connection to the App Store is needed to restore purchases.

Manage a subscription

Use Settings → Legal & Support → Manage Subscription, or manage subscriptions through your iPhone’s Apple Account settings. Deleting EchoGrid does not cancel a subscription.

Local progress

Progress is stored on your device. There is no EchoGrid account or in-app cloud-sync service. Settings → Reset all local progress removes saved puzzles, settings, and statistics. Resetting cannot be undone and does not cancel purchases.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy for information about local saves, Apple purchases, and support email.
